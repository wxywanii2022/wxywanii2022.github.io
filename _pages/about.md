<h1 class="welcome-lead">Welcome to my homepage!</h1>

I am an **M.S. student in Computer Science at Stanford University**. I earned my B.S. in Mathematics & Computer Science with a minor in Statistics from the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign (UIUC).

My research focuses on **large language models (LLMs)**, particularly model architectures, training methods, reasoning, and perception. I have also worked on **AI for science**. My broader goal is to build capable and reliable AI systems for complex real-world problems.
